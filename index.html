Beyond the Hospital

CareConnect began with hospital operations because that's where I experienced the challenges of coordinating people, patients, and operational work. As the project evolved, it became clear that many of those same coordination challenges exist across the broader healthcare system.

The goal is to build a flexible operational platform that can support different care environments while adapting to their unique workflows and responsibilities.

🏥 Acute Care Hospitals
🚑 Emergency Departments
🩺 Outpatient Clinics
🏃 Rehabilitation Hospitals
👵 Skilled Nursing Facilities
🏡 Long-Term Care & Assisted Living
🧠 Behavioral Health
🏥 Ambulatory Surgery Centers

While CareConnect currently showcases hospital operations, its underlying approach is designed to grow with the needs of healthcare organizations across the continuum of care.

How it was built

How I built it in Salesforce.

I used standard Salesforce tools for configuration and automation, then added Apex and Lightning Web Components where the requirements needed custom logic or UI.

Declarative Salesforce build Low-code configuration and automation
  • Custom objects, fields, relationships, and record types
  • Lightning App Builder and responsive record pages
  • Salesforce Flow, validation rules, routing, and automation
  • Permission sets, permission-set groups, reports, and dashboards
  • Sharing, navigation, tabs, and application configuration
Custom engineering Apex, LWC, JavaScript, and security logic
  • Apex services, controllers, and test classes
  • Lightning Web Components with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
  • SOQL, aggregate summaries, and performance-aware services
  • Facility authorization and CRUD/FLS enforcement
  • Location projection, live census, and lifecycle behavior
